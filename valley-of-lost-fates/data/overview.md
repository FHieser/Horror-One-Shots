## Premise

An ancient stone arch, filled with mist, leads into a hidden valley. Once through, there is no way back: from the inside, the arch stands empty.

Teams have been sent in before, and from them basic communications and gear were figured out. The players are the next expedition.

**Legacy.** The valley is played as a one-shot series. Once per game, the expedition can send a message of up to 20 words to the outside, and groups who play later may receive the messages of those who came before.

**The party.** Players build HeartCore characters as usual. A balanced group is recommended: ideally the Doctor, Mechanic, Scientist and Soldier are all covered, so the expedition can heal, fix, understand and fight.

## The Valley

At the centre stands the **Megalith**, the entry point into the valley. It connects every region, and it is where the cards are laid out. Around it lie the regions:

- **Plains:** the Village, the Fields, the Woods
- **Swamp:** the Witch Hut, the Abandoned Fishing Village
- **Sea:** the Lighthouse
- **Highlands:** Devilsrock, the Keep

Crossing a border costs **Resources** (see the [Map](./map.html)), and every character who crosses pays the full cost. **1 Resource** is either 1 Hope, 1 Stress, 1 HP or 1 Armor Slot, and a character can mix them. Route Stabilizers lower the cost (see [Gear](./gear.html)).

## The Cards

The valley changes with every game. At the start, the players meet the old lady on the Megalith and do the reading with her: the **22 major arcana** are shuffled with mixed orientation and drawn one by one, and the players place each card into a free slot of its colour around the map, without knowing what it means. The reading ends when every location slot is filled (record the reading on the [Map](./map.html); effects on [Cards](./cards.html)).

- **Yellow** cards are encounters at a single location.
- **Red** cards change the rules of a whole region.
- **Blue** cards affect the whole map.

## Goal

Find the **three pages**, the three readings the hermit once read at the cliff. The first card drawn in the reading goes into the white Pages field; it is set aside and decides where they lie, and each is gained by completing the challenge at its location, so a run never needs every quest. With all three, the final region beyond the Lighthouse opens: a whirlpool at the cliffs, where the meteor waits in the form of a monster (see [Final](./final.html)).

## Background

Centuries ago, a hermit and a fisher's daughter fell in love. She fell from the cliffs; he read his stories to her there three times, let the pages fall into the abyss, and finally followed her. Then a meteor came: a being from beyond time that had always been there. It took in the stories, and it changed itself and the valley. The valley's places are shaped by those stories (see [Lore](./lore.html)).
