# Rooms
This research facility is meant to research phenomena that create 
or connect to 3Dimensional Spaces or Dimensions.

# Basic Idea

Way in the past there once was a weird phase in the universe.
A timeframe of roughly 100 years that produced weird phenomena.

These phenomena could be anything from very unlikely things happening repeatedly like a lightning strike in the same place, to objects that gain weird properties to sentient beings that break the laws of reality.

These findings are discovered all over the universe and are collected to be studied and analyzed.

For that a company was founded.
One of its institutions is the one where this oneshot takes place.

This institution is located itself in a phenomena that allows for safe storage and disposal of individual phenomena, called the "Maw". 
Teeth at the end of a giant black hole. Anything thrown in there cant escape.
