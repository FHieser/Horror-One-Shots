01What Everyone Gets

Daedalus Group provides every crew member with:

  • Monthly salary (see role)
  • Crew attire
  • Food and accommodations
  • A pager
  • 1.5 kcr (thousand credits) starting money

02Choose Your Job

Each player picks one role. See the Roles tab for the details of each job.

03Who Are You?

Every player answers these. They have no mechanical effect; they are there to give the character a life outside the job.

  1. What do they do in their free time?
  2. How do they really relax?
  3. Why do they do this job?
  4. What is their dream?
  5. Draw the GM a door.

GM Only

  • Fill every role no player took with an NPC that you play.
  • Keep the door drawings. You will need them in Act I.
