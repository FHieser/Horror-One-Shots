# The Door

The Door defies understanding. It is a door, set into raw rock deep in the mines, where nothing should be. It does not want to be watched, and it draws people towards it.

## Observation

The core rule. Everything else grows from it.

- The Door **cannot act while it is observed**.
- Observation includes direct line of sight, live camera feeds and drone cameras.
- Several observers mean complete inaction.
- Unobserved, it can concentrate on **one person at a time**: omens, false pages, compulsions.

## Perception

Every character sees **a different door**: their own drawing from character creation. Where the handle is, what it is made of, what tech it seems to use. Describe each player's own drawing to them, and let them notice slowly that their descriptions don't match.

- If a player interacts with the Door in a way only they perceive (for example, reaching for a handle only they can see), **everyone else** gets a headache and makes a **Sanity Save**.
- This should become obvious in Act I, when the crew first visits the Door.

## Opening

The Door only opens when **nobody can see it**:

- the **lights are out**, and
- the **cameras are destroyed or switched off**.

When both are true, the player hears a **click** in the Door as it unlocks.

**Run it in secret.** Never discuss opening the Door openly at the table. Take the player into a separate room and let them try. Pager hints help, but don't be too obvious; the player should have an aha moment.

**Opening it means disappearing.** A player who opens the Door is **out of the game**. They must not tell the others what they know. They may still send **pager messages** to the remaining players through the GM, with no spoilers.

## Blowing It Up

An explosion at the Door releases a **massive pressure wave** that knocks out most people nearby. Whoever wakes up has to deal with the consequences: damaged suits, low air, the long way back up.

## Resistance

When the crew tries to lock the Door away or keep it from being reached:

- The next day, everyone gets a **nosebleed** and **+1 Stress**.
- The state of the mines **resets** to the previous day.
- Everyone makes a **Sanity Save**.

## Mass Interaction

If **three or more** people interact with the Door at the same time, it **disappears**.

## Solutions

There is no correct ending. These are the approaches players have tried or may try:

| Solution | Method | Outcome |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Leave it alone | Follow the captain's orders: watch it, keep the machines running, wait for the company | Delays, but the Door resists and keeps reaching out |
| Blow it up | Explosives at the Door or in the tunnel | Pressure wave knocks out everyone nearby |
| Open it | Lights out, cameras off, open alone | The player disappears |
| Mass interaction | Three or more people interact at once | The Door disappears |
| Wait it out | Cryo pods in the Med Bay | 6 pods; destroy some until players − 1 remain |
| Flee | Take the shuttle from the Hangar | Leaves others behind |
| Get creative | Player-driven ideas | Reward clever thinking |

Whatever happens, the epilogue on the [Overview](./) follows: two weeks later, the Daedalus security team arrives.
